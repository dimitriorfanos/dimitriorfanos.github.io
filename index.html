About

Engineer. Builder. Problem-solver.

I'm a process and R&D engineer with 4+ years of experience spanning R&D, systems test, instrumentation & controls, and hardware development. My work centers on building and operating complex hardware systems — instrumenting them, running experiments, troubleshooting failures, and making them reliable enough to scale.

Currently at MAAT Energy, I develop and operate pilot-scale microwave plasma reactors for syngas, hydrogen, and carbon production, while also building the SCADA and controls systems that keep them running reliably day-to-day.

I've worked across the full engineering loop: experimental design, sensor integration, automation, data analysis, root-cause investigation, and iterative hardware development. The domain has been plasma reactors, but the approach is general — build the system, understand the data, find what's breaking, and improve it until it works consistently.

I care about building systems that are measurable, robust, and ready to move beyond the lab.
